# Explanation

Explanation pages give the reasons behind Falco: why it exists, why it chooses one approach over another, how its parts fit together and what it deliberately does not do. Come here when you want to understand a decision or a mechanism, not when you need to perform a task or look up a value. Pages argue and may cite the measurements that support them; the figures themselves are in Reference.
